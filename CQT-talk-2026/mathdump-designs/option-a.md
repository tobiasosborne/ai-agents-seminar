# Option A: The Flood

**Pitch (about 150 words).** One particle per manuscript, 722 of them, on a dark full-bleed canvas. Colour shows the discipline: the three physics blocks (211-239, 260-284, 285-303) are one warm family, everything else is cool. Filled means the family has a Lean artefact; a ring means it has none. The same particles carry the whole sequence. They pour in day by day, flock into 17 discipline discs, drain through an attrition cascade that ends in a slammed "0 refereed", and then eight physics results fly out of the corpus as cards with Lean badges. The live beat is physical. The torrent of 23 and 24 Sep falls under gravity, bounces and settles, and the counters tick as the dots land. The mouse is a gentle gravity well, and R replays the pour. It suits CQT because the audience reads unit charts instantly and cares about what is actually verified. Risks: Inter is a system font on this laptop only, and the motion needs a decent GPU.

**Keys:** arrows or space as in the deck. R replays the pour on slide 1. Moving the mouse bends nearby particles (cosmetic). `?still` and print render every slide at its final state.

## Slides and build steps

### 1. `s-md-flood`: "The trickle became a flood" (4 steps)
- **0:** Empty date axis, 10 Sep to 6 Oct. Counters read 0. *Say:* "Every dot will be one manuscript, placed by the date in its folder name."
- **1:** The trickle. 7 manuscripts drip in between 10 and 22 Sep. *Say:* "For two weeks, a handful."
- **2:** The torrent. 177 manuscripts fall on 23 Sep and 194 on 24 Sep. The manuscript and family counters race. *Pause and let it fall.*
- **3:** The rest arrives day by day, including the 112 of 5 Oct. "51% landed on 23 and 24 Sep." Counters stop at 722 and 372.
- **4:** 34,815 PDF pages and 26.0M lines of Lean count up. "About 4,000 problems posed, ~3 h of compute per result."
- *Note:* the Sep 24 column has 194 dots: 193 dated folders plus 1 ISO-named folder (2026-09-24), per stats.md.

### 2. `s-md-disc`: "17 disciplines" (2 steps)
- **0:** The particles flock into 17 discs sized by manuscript count. The three physics blocks sit together on the bottom row. *Say:* "This is not a niche dump. It is all of mathematics."
- **1:** The physics blocks glow and the rest dims: "73 families, 195 manuscripts".
- **2:** Lean coverage. Rings dim and the labels switch to "% with Lean". "Often a fragment, not the headline. MUB(6) = 3: the Lean covers only N(6) ≤ 5."

### 3. `s-md-cascade`: "What survives the checks" (4 steps)
- **0:** The ~4,000-problem column runs off the top of the screen. 372 family dots stand next to it.
- **1:** Families split into 722 manuscripts. The column of families stays as a ghost.
- **2:** Manuscripts with Lean move on and merge into 235 family dots. The rest settle under gravity. *Say:* "Lean artefacts are often a weak fragment."
- **3:** A thin stream, "a handful, by hand count", for headline fully in Lean. The examples (273, 274, 275, 287, 288, 295, 296 and a few more) were checked in the physics blocks only.
- **4:** The red 0 slams into an empty tray, the stage shakes and the particles ripple. "Refereed: by anyone, as of 7 Oct." *Stop talking for a beat.*

### 4. `s-md-picks`: "The physicist's picks" (5 steps)
- **0:** The whole corpus as one band in family order. "The warm stretch is the physics."
- **1:** 265 area law (Lean: §2 only) and 268 Haldane gap (no Lean, computer-assisted) fly out with their tethers.
- **2:** 271 Heisenberg ferromagnet (Lean: order only) and 269 Laughlin gap (Lean: no disorder, computer-assisted).
- **3:** 266 MUBs in d=6 (Lean: N(6) ≤ 5 only, computer-assisted) and 273 entropy photon-number inequality (Lean: headline).
- **4:** 274 parity ∉ QAC⁰ (Lean: headline) and 275 QMA-hard Coulomb energy (Lean: both theorems).
- **5:** "3 of 8 have the headline in Lean. The rest need a referee."

### 5. `s-md-close`: closing observation (4 steps)
- **0:** The particles turn slowly as a dim galaxy. Kicker: "The first week of reactions".
- **1:** Sutherland: "We should ask for receipts."
- **2:** Isaac Kim: "a shocking list of problems in quantum information..."
- **3:** AGMAI: "should not be used as a marketing tool". They ask for prompts, compute per result, and a repository that no AI lab controls.
- **4:** "The bottleneck moved from producing claims to adjudicating them." Sub-line: "722 manuscripts in four weeks. Refereed so far: none."
