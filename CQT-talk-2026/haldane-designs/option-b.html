Haldane gap, family 268 · 5 of 5

What is actually proved

The paper's own plane: two seeds, two staircases, no toy. Constants as stated in the paper and checked by the review.

Theorem 1.1

HL = Σj Sj·Sj+1, spin 1, periodic. Even L ≥ 60: unique ground state and γL > (4/105) log(80/79) ≈ 4.8 × 10−4. Even L ≥ 2304: γL > log 20/784 ≈ 3.8 × 10−3. Hence liminf γL > 0.

The seeds, where the labour is

Seed at 60: exact Zn for n ≤ 10 at β = 21/2 (59,049 states, exact integers), moment filters on the spatial spectrum, a bond-dimension-26 MPS giving Z120 > 1, a 5/2-power raise to β = 105/4. Margin: S(60, 105/4) > 7/8 by 6 × 10−4.

Seed at 2304: Zn for n ≤ 12 at β = 49/4; defects 0.048 and 0.18 at (36, 49/4), six updates bring both below 0.01 at (2304, 784).

Verification status

No Lean. Exact-rational certificates: integer tables with a proven truncation error, big-integer MPS contraction, fractions for every scalar. Re-run by the review: both scalar scripts, both contractions, independent diagonalisation for n ≤ 9. Unverified: the n = 10 totals at 21/2, needed for L ≥ 60, and n = 10 to 12 at 49/4. Reviewer's estimate: 85 to 90%.

Honest caveats

Even periodic rings and the liminf of their gaps, not uniqueness of every infinite-volume ground state. The constant is a thousand times below the true gap of about 0.41. Nothing uses spin 1 or frustration-freeness: it is a finite-size RG-flow criterion.
