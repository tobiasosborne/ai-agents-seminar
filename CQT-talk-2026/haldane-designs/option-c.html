The fine print
what is proved, what was checked, where a bug would hide
Theorem 1.1
HL = Σj Sj·Sj+1, spin 1, periodic. Every even L ≥ 60: unique ground state and γL > (4/105) log(80/79) ≈ 4.8·10−4. Every even L ≥ 2304: γL > log 20 / 784 ≈ 3.8·10−3. Hence liminf γL > 0.
Scope
Even periodic rings and the liminf of their gaps. Cor 6.1 gives a gap inequality for subsequential limits of periodic ground states, not uniqueness or a gap for every infinite-volume ground state. True gap ≈ 0.41: the constants are a thousand times smaller.
The seeds
Where the labour is. (60, 105/4) from exact Zn, n ≤ 10, at β = 21/2 (59,049 states, exact integers, rigorous series truncation), polynomial filters on the spectrum, a bond-dimension-26 MPS giving Z120 > 1, a 5/2-power raise of β. Margin: S(60,105/4) > 7/8 by 6·10−4. Second seed (36, 49/4): defects 0.048 and 0.18, six doublings to (2304, 784).
Verification
No Lean. Exact-rational scalar scripts and both MPS contractions re-run and pass; tightest margins 4.7·10−5 and 6·10−4. Independent exact diagonalisation reproduces the tables for n ≤ 9. Unverified: Z10(21/2), which the L ≥ 60 theorem needs, and n = 10 to 12 at β = 49/4.
Verdict
One reviewer, one pass: no mathematical error found. 85 to 90% that the theorem is proved as written, conditional on the n = 10 to 12 integer totals. If a bug hides anywhere, it is in the seeds, not in the doubling.
